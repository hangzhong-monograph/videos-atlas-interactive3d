# Spatial Interfaces of Innate Immunity

### Videos · Microscopy Atlas · Interactive 3D

A visual research companion exploring the spatial organization of innate immune responses, with particular emphasis on macrophage–cell interactions, complement-associated membrane processes, and cellular ultrastructure.

This project is being developed as a companion resource to a doctoral monograph at the University of Helsinki.

## Research Themes

The website is organized around two complementary areas of innate immunity:

- **Macrophage–cell interfaces:** cellular contacts, membrane exchange, and structural changes during infection.
- **Complement–erythrocyte interfaces:** complement-associated membrane interactions and cellular ultrastructure.
